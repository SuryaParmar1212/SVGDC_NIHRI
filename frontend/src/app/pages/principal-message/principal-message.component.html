Principal’s Message

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Government Degree College, Nihri, an institution guided by the noble motto “अप्प दीपो भव:” – “Be a Light Unto Yourself.” Situated in a predominantly rural region, the college stands as a beacon of hope and opportunity for students who aspire to pursue higher education despite social, economic, and geographical challenges.

The college plays a significant role in extending the benefits of higher education to the rural youth of the region, with a majority of our students being girls. We firmly believe that educating a girl is synonymous with empowering a family and strengthening society. Our institution remains deeply committed to promoting women’s education, self-reliance, and confidence, enabling students to emerge as informed, responsible, and capable citizens.

At Government Degree College, Nihri, we strive to provide an inclusive, safe, and nurturing academic environment for students from economically weaker sections and first-generation learners. Our dedicated faculty members work tirelessly to ensure quality education through student-centric teaching, academic mentoring, and holistic development. Alongside academic excellence, we emphasize ethical values, discipline, and social responsibility.

I encourage our students to make the best use of the opportunities available, to develop resilience and curiosity, and to pursue excellence in all spheres of life. Together, let us continue to transform lives through education and work towards building a brighter and empowered future for our rural community.

With warm regards and best wishes.

Ms. Seema Sharma

Principal

Government Degree College, Nihri
