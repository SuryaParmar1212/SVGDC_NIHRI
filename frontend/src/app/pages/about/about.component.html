Our History

Established in 1950, Government college began with just three departments and 150 students. Over the years, we have grown exponentially, now serving over 5,000 students across 25+ departments.

Our journey has been marked by continuous growth, academic achievements, and unwavering commitment to educational excellence, making us a preferred destination for higher studies.
