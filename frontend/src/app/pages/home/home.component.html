Introduction of the College

"अप्प दीपो भव:" – "Be a Light Unto Yourself."

Government Degree College, Nihri, Tehsil Nihri, District Mandi (Himachal Pradesh), was established in 2014 by the Government of Himachal Pradesh. The institution is committed to nurturing academic excellence, self-reliance, and a strong sense of social responsibility among its students.

Set amidst serene and pristine natural surroundings, the college offers a breathtaking panoramic view of majestic mountain peaks, lush forested hillsides, and picturesque rural hamlets. This tranquil and pollution-free environment provides an ideal setting for intellectual growth and holistic development.

Situated in a predominantly rural area, the college serves as an important hub of higher education for the surrounding region. A distinctive feature is that a majority of its students are girls, reflecting the college’s commitment to women’s education, empowerment, and social upliftment through accessible, inclusive, and quality education.

Our Vision

Government Degree College, Nihri envisions itself as a catalyst for educational, social, and economic transformation in the rural region it serves. The institution is committed to creating an inclusive, equitable, and learner-centric educational environment, with special focus on students from rural areas, economically disadvantaged backgrounds, and a majority of girl students.

By upholding the highest standards of education, the college aims to empower young women and first-generation learners with knowledge, skills, values, and confidence.

Our Mission

  • To provide accessible, affordable, and quality higher education to students from rural and economically weaker sections.
  • To promote women’s education and empowerment by creating a safe, supportive, and encouraging academic environment for girl students.
  • To adopt innovative and student-centric teaching–learning practices that foster academic excellence, critical thinking, and lifelong learning.
  • To inculcate ethical values, social responsibility, and self-reliance among students.
  • To support the holistic development of learners through academic, cultural, and co-curricular activities.
